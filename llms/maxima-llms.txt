# Maxima

> Maxima is an AI-native accounting and financial-close automation platform for enterprise finance teams. Its AI agents automate journal entries, cash coding, payroll entries, bank and balance-sheet reconciliations, flux (variance) analysis, and fixed-asset depreciation, connecting natively to the ERPs, banks, and payroll systems finance teams already use (150+ integrations advertised). Backed by Kleiner Perkins. As of this profile, Maxima publishes no public developer API or documentation; the product is delivered as a gated web application.
